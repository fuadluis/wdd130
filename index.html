Texas

Texas is a state in the southern United States. It is bordered by Mexico on the south, Louisiana and part of Arkansas on the east, Oklahoma is to the north. It also touches New Mexico on the east. Texas nickname is The Lone Star state and it has a cowboy culture. It is the second largest state in the US. Texas is the only state that can fly their flag at the same height as the USA.
